## Cold Coffee Spotlight

**Status:** Ready to plan

**Objective:** Promote the cafe's top-selling revenue driver to encourage higher sales and attract cafe visitors.

**Target Audience:** Existing customers and cafe visitors.

**Supporting Evidence:** Cold Coffee generated Rs. 204,588.00 in sales and 1,139 units sold, making it the leading revenue product.

**Marketing Channel:** Instagram, WhatsApp, and in-store menu display.

**Campaign Message:** Your cafe favourite is here! Try our Cold Coffee on your next visit.

**Budget:** Estimated allocation: ₹1,000 to ₹2,000 from the monthly marketing budget of INR 8,000 (Subject to owner approval).

**Success Measurement:** Track Cold Coffee sales volume, total revenue generated from the item, and customer engagement on social media posts.

**Owner Approval:** Campaign publishing, promotional messaging, and budget allocation require approval from the business owner.

## Cafe Happy Hours

**Status:** Ready to plan

**Objective:** Encourage customer visits during quieter hours to balance out peak-hour operational strain.

**Target Audience:** Customers visiting during quieter hours (outside of peak hours like 13:00).

**Supporting Evidence:** Peak sales happen around 13:00, with Rs. 206,360.00 in recorded sales, while the business doctor noted the need to manage peak-hour service and improve off-peak utilisation.

**Marketing Channel:** Instagram Stories and in-store posters.

**Campaign Message:** Take a little coffee break! Discover your favourite cafe treats during our relaxed hours.

**Execution Steps:**
1. Identify the quietest operating hours of the day (excluding 13:00 and 19:00 peaks).
2. Design in-store posters and Instagram Stories inviting patrons to enjoy a relaxed coffee break.
3. Display the posters near the entrance and tables.

**Budget:** Estimated allocation: ₹1,000 from the monthly marketing budget of INR 8,000 (Subject to owner approval).

**Success Measurement:** Monitor customer visits and sales counts during the designated off-peak hours.

**Owner Approval:** Campaign publishing, promotional timing, and budget allocation require approval from the business owner.

## Come Back for Another Cup

**Status:** Ready to plan

**Objective:** Increase repeat visits and build customer retention among existing patrons.

**Target Audience:** Existing and repeat customers, aligning with business goals to increase repeat visits.

**Supporting Evidence:** A retention campaign can encourage repeat visits without relying solely on new customer acquisition, supporting the core business goal to increase repeat visits.

**Marketing Channel:** WhatsApp and printed loyalty cards.

**Campaign Message:** Your next favourite coffee is waiting! Visit us again and enjoy a little something on your journey to becoming a regular.

**Execution Steps:**
1. Design a simple printed loyalty card for returning customers.
2. Share reminders and updates via WhatsApp broadcasts to existing customer contacts who opt-in.

**Budget:** Estimated allocation: ₹2,000 for printing loyalty cards from the monthly marketing budget of INR 8,000 (Subject to owner approval).

**Success Measurement:** Track the number of loyalty cards distributed, completed cards redeemed, and the frequency of repeat customer visits.
