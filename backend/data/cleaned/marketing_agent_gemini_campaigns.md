## Cold Coffee Spotlight

**Status:** Ready to plan

**Objective:** Feature Cafe Nova's highest revenue-generating product (Cold Coffee) to maintain sales momentum and attract customer interest.

**Target Audience:** Existing customers, cafe visitors, college students, and young professionals.

**Supporting Evidence:** Cold Coffee is the top-selling product by revenue, generating Rs. 204,588.00 and 1,139 units sold.

**Marketing Channel:** Instagram, WhatsApp, and in-store menu highlights.

**Campaign Message:** Your cafe favourite is here! Try our Cold Coffee on your next visit.

**Execution Steps:** 
1. Feature Cold Coffee prominently on the in-store menu boards.
2. Share visually appealing photos of Cold Coffee on the cafe's Instagram feed and WhatsApp status updates.
3. Train counter staff to mention the Cold Coffee when taking customer orders.

**Budget:** Estimated allocation of INR 2,000 from the monthly marketing budget of INR 8,000 (subject to owner approval).

**Success Measurement:** Track the volume of Cold Coffee units sold and monitor overall beverage sales during the campaign period.

**Owner Approval:** Required for budget allocation and publishing social media posts.

## Cafe Happy Hours

**Status:** Ready to plan

**Objective:** Attract customers during quieter hours to balance out customer flow and improve off-peak sales.

**Target Audience:** Customers visiting during quieter hours, nearby residents, and students.

**Supporting Evidence:** Peak sales happen around 13:00, with Rs. 206,360.00 in recorded sales, while other hours experience lower traffic and recurring complaints regarding service and waiting times during peak slots.

**Marketing Channel:** Instagram Stories and in-store posters.

**Campaign Message:** Take a little coffee break! Discover your favourite cafe treats during our relaxed hours.

**Execution Steps:**
1. Design simple in-store table tents or posters promoting the quieter hours of the cafe.
2. Publish Instagram Stories before and during off-peak windows to invite followers in for a relaxed visit.
3. Ensure staff manage table availability effectively during these relaxed windows.

**Budget:** Estimated allocation of INR 2,000 from the monthly marketing budget of INR 8,000 (subject to owner approval).

**Success Measurement:** Monitor customer visits and sales volume during off-peak hours compared to baseline data.

**Owner Approval:** Required for promotional timing and marketing material approval.

## Come Back for Another Cup

**Status:** Ready to plan

**Objective:** Encourage customer retention and repeat visits from existing patrons without relying solely on new customer acquisition.

**Target Audience:** Existing and repeat customers visiting Cafe Nova.

**Supporting Evidence:** Business goals specify a focus on increasing repeat visits, supported by a loyal customer base among college students and young professionals.

**Marketing Channel:** WhatsApp and printed loyalty cards.

**Campaign Message:** Your next favourite coffee is waiting! Visit us again and enjoy a little something on your journey to becoming a regular.

**Execution Steps:**
1. Print simple return-visit reward cards for in-store distribution.
2. Send friendly reminders via WhatsApp to existing customer lists about the repeat-visit appreciation card.
3. Train staff to stamp or punch loyalty cards upon purchase completion.

**Budget:** Estimated allocation of INR 2,500 from the monthly marketing budget of INR 8,000 for printing loyalty cards and WhatsApp messaging (subject to owner approval).

**Success Measurement:** Track the number of completed loyalty cards redeemed and the frequency of repeat customer visits.

**Owner Approval:** Required for printing loyalty cards, setting loyalty terms, and messaging approval.

## Clean Cafe, Happy Customers

**Status:** Hold until improvements are verified

**Objective:** Address recurring customer dissatisfaction regarding cleanliness and communicate improvements only after they are fully implemented.

**Target Audience:** All cafe customers visiting the premises.

**Supporting Evidence:** Cleanliness is the most reported customer complaint theme, with 45 complaint mentions noted by the Business Doctor.

**Marketing Channel:** In-store signage and social media (to be used *only* after verification).

**Campaign Message:** We care about your comfort. We're making every visit welcoming and enjoyable.

**Execution Steps:**
1. **Operational Improvement Task:** The business owner and staff review cleaning schedules, establish routine cleanliness checks, and monitor table turnover during busy periods.
2. **Owner Verification:** The owner personally inspects and verifies that cleaning routines have been successfully updated and maintained.
3. **Customer-Facing Communication:** Only after verification, place subtle in-store signage and update social media channels to reflect the renewed commitment to cleanliness.

**Budget:** Estimated allocation of INR 1,500 from the monthly marketing budget of INR 8,000 for verified in-store communication materials (subject to owner approval).

**Success Measurement:** Track the reduction of cleanliness-related mentions in future customer reviews and feedback forms.

**Owner Approval:** Required for operational cleaning schedule changes, budget allocation, and publishing communication about cleanliness.

*(Note: The "French Fries Quality First" campaign is on hold and excluded from active customer-facing promotion until its quality concerns have been investigated and addressed, in compliance with safety rules.)*

# MARKETING AGENT SUMMARY

1. **Best campaign to start with:** **Cold Coffee Spotlight** is the best campaign to start with because it leverages an existing bestseller (Cold Coffee generated Rs. 204,588.00 in sales) with high customer demand, requiring minimal risk and straightforward execution.
2. **Campaigns that should wait for operational improvements:** **Clean Cafe, Happy Customers** must wait until the business owner physically verifies that cleaning routines and table checks have been successfully improved. Additionally, the French Fries quality campaign remains on hold pending internal recipe and preparation reviews.
3. **Important precautions for the cafe owner:** 
   - Never promote French Fries until quality concerns (18 negative reviews) have been fully investigated and resolved.
   - Do not advertise cleanliness improvements as completed before verifying them on-site.
   - Monitor staff workloads during peak hours (especially around 13:00) to mitigate service and waiting time complaints (40 service complaints, 37 waiting time complaints).
   - Never exceed the stated monthly marketing budget of INR 8,000, and ensure all spending and discounts receive explicit owner approval.
4. **How Growth360 can measure campaign performance:** Growth360 can track performance by monitoring product sales volumes, tracking changes in customer review sentiment, observing complaint theme frequencies (such as cleanliness, service, and waiting times), and measuring repeat customer visits over time against synthetic baseline records.
