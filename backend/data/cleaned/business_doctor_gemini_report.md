## 5. Root Cause Investigation
* **Observation:** French Fries is associated with a high volume of negative feedback.
  * **Supporting Evidence:** 18 negative reviews and an average rating of 3.42 out of 5 from 69 reviews.
  * **What still needs to be checked:** The specific preparation steps, ingredients, quality, and presentation of French Fries require internal investigation to understand the driver behind the negative feedback.
* **Observation:** High customer complaints regarding waiting times and service coincide with peak operating hours.
  * **Supporting Evidence:** 40 service complaints, 37 waiting time complaints, and peak sales volume occurring at 13:00 (Rs. 206,360.00 and 1,292 units).
  * **What still needs to be checked:** Whether current staffing levels, order preparation workflows, and table turnover procedures are adequately matched to customer volume during peak hours.

## 6. Recommended Actions
* **Action 1: Review Cleaning Routines and Table Turnover**
  * **Priority:** High
  * **What the owner should do:** Review cleaning schedules, establish routine cleanliness checks, and monitor table turnover during busy periods.
  * **Why it is recommended:** Cleanliness is the single most reported complaint theme (45 mentions) impacting customer satisfaction.
  * **How the owner can measure improvement:** Track the frequency of cleanliness-related mentions in future customer reviews.
* **Action 2: Investigate French Fries Quality**
  * **Priority:** High
  * **What the owner should do:** Investigate the preparation, quality, and presentation of French Fries. (Note: As per rules, do not promote this product before addressing quality concerns).
  * **Why it is recommended:** French Fries is the most negatively reviewed product, holding 18 negative reviews and a 3.42/5 rating.
  * **How the owner can measure improvement:** Monitor changes in product-specific ratings and negative feedback volume for French Fries.
* **Action 3: Review Staffing and Preparation Arrangements for Peak Hours**
  * **Priority:** Medium
  * **What the owner should do:** Review staffing levels, kitchen preparation workflows, and cleaning arrangements specifically before the 13:00 peak sales hour (requiring owner approval for any staffing or cost changes).
  * **Why it is recommended:** Hour 13 generates the highest sales activity (Rs. 206,360.00), which correlates with high customer complaints regarding waiting times and service.
  * **How the owner can measure improvement:** Track waiting time and service complaint counts during peak operating hours.
* **Action 4: Monitor Stock Availability for High-Revenue Products**
  * **Priority:** Medium
  * **What the owner should do:** Monitor inventory and stock availability for top-performing items like Cold Coffee, Grilled Cheese Sandwich, Paneer Wrap, Cappuccino, and Iced Latte.
  * **Why it is recommended:** These core products drive substantial revenue and volume (e.g., Cold Coffee generated Rs. 204,588.00).
  * **How the owner can measure improvement:** Maintain steady sales volumes and prevent stockout occurrences for these top items.

## 7. Final Advice to the Cafe Owner
Cafe Nova demonstrates strong sales demand during peak afternoon and evening hours, supported by popular core products like Cold Coffee and Cappuccino. However, customer feedback highlights clear operational pain points around cleanliness, waiting times, and specific menu items like French Fries. By systematically investigating product quality and fine-tuning peak-hour operations, the cafe has a solid foundation to improve its overall business health and customer satisfaction.
